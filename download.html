First run

From install to first clip in about a minute.

  1. Open the app and sign in. Email and password is fastest. Google sign-in is available where configured.
  2. Paste a live link. Twitch, YouTube or Kick — the channel page URL is fine.
  3. Press Start clipping. It spends the first minute learning the channel's normal. Nothing fires before that.
  4. Wait for the first clip. The first one downloads the speech model (about 140 MB) — later clips are faster.
  5. Connect YouTube or TikTok on the Accounts page if you want to post from the app.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

macOS says "Motion Mania is damaged and can't be opened"

That is Gatekeeper reacting to a downloaded app. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway". If it still refuses, re-download the latest build.

No clips are appearing

Give it a minute after pressing Start — it learns the channel's baseline first. Check that the stream is actually live, that chat is readable, and that the log at the bottom of Home isn't reporting a stream error.

Rendering is slow

Close other heavy apps, and check whether a hardware encoder is available. 60 fps and the High preset cost the most. A 30-second clip should take well under a minute on a recent laptop.

The phone app won't connect

The phone app needs the same network path as your computer. Make sure the tunnel shows a green address in the Phone tab, then scan the QR code again. If you restarted the app, the address may have changed — re-scan.

Where are my clips stored?

On your computer, in the app's buffer folder. "Open clips folder" at the bottom of the Home screen takes you there. Uninstalling the app leaves them in place unless you delete that folder.
