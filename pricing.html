Billing

Questions about paying

How do I pay?

Card payments are handled by our merchant of record, so sales tax and VAT are calculated automatically. You never enter card details into Motion Mania.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, from your account or the renewal reminder email. It takes the same number of steps as signing up — no phone call or support ticket. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then clipping keeps working on the Free plan.

Do you offer refunds?

If something is broken and we can't fix it, write to us within 14 days of a charge and we'll refund it. See the terms of service.

Will I be charged automatically?

Pro renews at the interval you chose. We email you at least 7 days before each renewal, and you can cancel in one step from the email or your account. There is no free trial, so no surprise first charge.

Why is it cheaper than other clippers?

Because we don't rent GPUs. Editing and rendering happen on your computer, so we don't have to charge by the minute or cap how many clips you make. You pay for the software; your machine does the work.

Do I need to pay to try it?

No. The Free plan is permanent, not a trial. You can clip, edit and download as much as you like. There is no free trial that auto-charges later.

Is there a student or creator discount?

Ask us. Small channels and students — email support@motionmania.app from the address on your channel and we'll work something out.
