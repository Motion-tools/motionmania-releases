Upgrade inside the app.

We keep checkout in the desktop app on purpose. Pro is tied to the account you sign into, so there is no separate email field to get wrong and no licence key to copy.

  2. Sign in with the account you want to keep.
  3. Go to Account & Pro.
  4. Choose monthly ($15).
  5. Secure checkout opens in your browser; Pro activates automatically when payment completes.
