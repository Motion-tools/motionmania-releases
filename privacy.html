Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Motion Mania is a desktop application that clips live streams. This policy explains what we collect, what stays on your computer, and what you can delete.

The short version: your video files never leave your computer. We collect the minimum needed for an account, a licence and optional AI features. We do not sell data, and this website uses no advertising or analytics cookies.

1. Data that stays on your computer

  • Recorded streams and clips. Recorded footage, rendered clips, thumbnails and edit history are stored in the app's local buffer folder. They are not uploaded to us.
  • Connected account tokens. If you connect YouTube or TikTok, the access and refresh tokens are stored on your computer and encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Keychain on macOS, DPAPI on Windows). They are sent only to the platform they belong to.
  • Passwords. If you create an email and password account, we store only a scrypt hash and salt. We never store the password itself.
  • Per-install secrets. Session-signing keys and push-notification keys are generated on your machine and stay there.

2. Data we collect on our servers

When you create an account or subscribe, we store:

  • Your email address and display name
  • Your plan (Free, Pro), subscription status and expiry date
  • A subscription identifier from our payment provider (never your card number)
  • Counters needed to enforce plan limits, such as how many AI requests an account has made in the current month
  • Basic technical logs (time, IP address, request type) used for security and abuse prevention

We do not store your stream recordings, your clips, your transcripts, or your platform tokens on our servers.

3. AI features

Pro plans include AI-written titles and an AI quality check. Our AI provider is Anthropic (the Claude models). When these run, the app sends the following through our proxy:

  • Up to three still frames from the candidate clip
  • The transcript of the clip
  • A sample of chat messages, as text only, without usernames

It does not send the video file. We do not use this content to train models, and we do not keep copies of prompts or responses beyond short-lived operational logs. Anthropic processes the request under its own terms. If you are on the Free plan, or AI features are switched off, this data is not sent at all.

4. Google (YouTube) data

If you connect a YouTube channel, Motion Mania requests the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes, plus basic sign-in scopes. It uses them only to:

  • Identify which channel is connected
  • Upload videos that you have explicitly approved

Motion Mania's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to third parties except as necessary to provide the upload feature or as required by law. You can disconnect at any time in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. TikTok data

If you connect TikTok, Motion Mania receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and open ID). It uses the upload scope only to send videos you approved to your TikTok inbox or drafts. It does not read your videos, followers, messages or analytics. You can revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services, or by disconnecting in Motion Mania.

6. Payments

Payments are processed by a merchant of record (for example Paddle). They handle card details, tax and invoicing. We receive a subscription status and an identifier, not your card number.

Pro renews automatically at the interval you chose. We email a renewal reminder at least 7 days before each charge, and you can cancel from your account or that email in one step. There is no free trial, so there is no surprise first charge.

7. Third-party processors

We use a small number of providers, only for the purpose shown:

  • Anthropic — AI titles and the AI quality check for Pro users (clip transcript, still frames and sampled chat text; never the video file).
  • Paddle (merchant of record) — card payments, tax, invoices and subscription status. We never receive your card number.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, DNS, the private phone-app tunnel and basic security logs (IP address, time, request type).
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) — delivers “clip ready” notifications to your phone. The payload is a clip title and ID, not the video.
  • Google and TikTok — only when you connect an account, and only to upload the clips you approve.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising.

8. The phone app

The phone web app is served from your own computer through a private tunnel. Clips, thumbnails and edit actions travel between your phone and your computer; they are not routed through our servers. Push notifications are delivered by your browser's push service using keys generated on your computer.

9. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It may use your browser's local storage for basic interface preferences. The desktop app does not include third-party analytics.

10. Legal bases and your rights

Where data protection law applies (for example GDPR or UK GDPR), we process account and billing data to perform our contract with you, and security logs on the basis of our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. You may request access, correction, export or deletion of your data, and you may object to processing. Contact us using the details below. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are in California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

11. Retention

  • Account and licence data: kept while your account exists, then deleted within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Billing records: kept as long as tax law requires.
  • Security logs: kept for up to 90 days.
  • AI counters: reset monthly; totals are kept for billing integrity.

12. Security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. Platform tokens are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage. Passwords are salted and hashed with scrypt. Phone sessions are signed, expire after 30 days, and are revoked when you sign out on your computer. We keep security logs for a limited time and do not log AI request bodies. No system is perfect; if you find a vulnerability, please report it privately using the contact page.

13. Children

Motion Mania is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

14. International transfers

Our servers and providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

15. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you by email.

16. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@motionmania.app. See also how to delete your data.
