FAQ

Free clipping tool questions

Is Motion Mania really free?

Yes. The free plan clips, edits, captions and exports 1080×1920 MP4 files with no watermark and no credits. Pro adds Pro Edit, Storyline, the phone app, AI titles and up to three live streams at once.

What does “no credits” mean?

Cloud clippers charge by the minute of video or by the clip. Motion Mania renders on your computer, so there is no meter, no monthly clip quota and nothing to top up.

Which platforms does it clip?

Twitch, YouTube and Kick live streams, plus past broadcasts and normal videos. Local video files work too.

Do I need an account?

Not for the free plan. Sign in when you want Pro, the phone app or publishing.

Does it upload my footage?

No. Detection, editing and rendering happen locally. The network is used for the stream itself and optional AI titles on Pro.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The free plan is permanent, not a trial, and there is no card to download.

Download it and clip something

No account needed to try. Pro is a switch inside the app.
