How it works

From a GTA 6 stream to a vertical clip

1. Paste the stream link

A Twitch, YouTube or Kick live link — the channel URL is enough. For an ended stream, use the VOD link.

2. Let it watch

It learns the channel's normal for about a minute, then starts clipping when chat and audio jump.

3. Edit and export

Review the clip, fix captions by hand if you want, then export a 1080×1920 MP4. No watermark.

FAQ

GTA 6 clipping questions

Can I clip GTA 6 with Motion Mania?

Yes. Point Motion Mania at a GTA 6 live stream or VOD on Twitch, YouTube or Kick, and it cuts vertical clips with captions. Only clip footage you own or have permission to republish.

Does it spoil the GTA 6 story?

You decide what goes in. The game-style edit has a spoiler-safe switch that skips cinematic styling when a moment looks like a story beat, and the AI title rules avoid naming missions or in-game characters.

Is it free?

The free plan edits, captions and exports 1080×1920 MP4 files with no watermark or credits. Pro adds Pro Edit, Storyline, the phone app, AI titles and up to three streams at once.

Does my computer need to stay on?

Yes. Recording, clipping and rendering happen locally, which is why there is no per-minute fee and your footage never leaves the machine.

Does it work with GTA 6 streams on Twitch, YouTube and Kick?

Yes, all three. Past broadcasts and normal videos work too, so you can clip a finished stream after it ends.

No. Motion Mania is an independent editing tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
