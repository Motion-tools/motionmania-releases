Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains when and how Motion Mania Pro purchases can be refunded. It applies to subscriptions bought inside the Motion Mania desktop app. Our payment provider is the merchant of record and handles payment processing, tax and refunds.

Motion Mania is operated by Pritam Sapkota, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada.

1. 14-day refund window

If Motion Mania Pro is materially broken and we cannot fix it, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we will refund that charge. “Materially broken” means a core paid feature does not work as described and support cannot resolve it in a reasonable time.

This does not limit any statutory consumer rights you have where you live.

2. Duplicate or unauthorised charges

If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or believe a charge was unauthorised, contact us with the email on the account and the approximate charge date. We will investigate and refund any confirmed error.

3. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from the app or from the payment provider customer portal or renewal email. Cancelling stops future renewals. Pro normally remains active until the end of the period you already paid for; we do not automatically refund the unused part unless required by law or the service is materially broken.

4. What is not automatically refundable

  • Change of mind after the 14-day window.
  • Not using the app, or deciding the free plan is enough.
  • Platform decisions such as a YouTube/TikTok strike, removal, monetisation change or account suspension.
  • Content you did not have the right to clip or publish.
  • A feature that works as described but does not produce a specific number of views or go viral.

We may still approve a refund outside these rules at our discretion, especially for accidental purchases or genuine hardship.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@motionmania.app from the email address on your Motion Mania account. Include:

  • the account email
  • the approximate charge date and amount
  • what went wrong, and any error message or screenshot

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through the payment provider. Bank processing can take 5–10 business days after approval.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a chargeback. A chargeback can suspend access while the bank investigates. We will work with the payment provider to resolve a genuine billing error quickly.

7. Contact

support@motionmania.app
