Last updated: 16 November 2026

Motion Mania is desktop video editing software. It does not provide, host, stream, download or distribute third-party video content. It is a tool for editing footage you already have the right to use.

You may import, record or edit only content that you own or have clear, documented permission to use and republish. A public stream, video or social post is not automatically licensed for reuse.

If you edit someone else's broadcast, you need their permission and any permission required by the platform where it was published. If you are unsure, do not use it.

If you believe content made with Motion Mania infringes your rights, email support@motionmania.app with enough detail to identify the content and your rights. We will review valid notices and take appropriate action.
