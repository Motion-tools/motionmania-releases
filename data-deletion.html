Delete your data

Last updated: 7 November 2026

There are two kinds of data to delete: the data on your own computer, and the small amount we hold on our servers. You can remove both.

1. Delete local data (all plans)

Your recordings, clips, thumbnails and edit history live in the app's buffer folder on your computer.

  1. Open Motion Mania → Home → Storage → Delete all clips. This removes every recording, clip, thumbnail and phone cache on your computer, including approved clips. It cannot be undone.
  2. Or click Open clips folder at the bottom of the Home screen to delete individual files.
  3. Uninstall Motion Mania. On macOS, drag it to the Bin. On Windows, use Settings → Apps → Motion Mania → Uninstall.

If you also want to remove saved settings and connected-account tokens, delete the app's data folder:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Motion Mania
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\Motion Mania

Deleting that folder removes the account session, encrypted platform tokens and per-install keys from your machine.

2. Disconnect YouTube or TikTok

This revokes our access without deleting anything else:

  • In the app: Accounts → the connected platform → Disconnect.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Motion Mania → Remove access.
  • TikTok: Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services → Motion Mania → Remove.

3. Delete your account and server data

Email support@motionmania.app from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my Motion Mania account”.

We will:

  • Confirm the request within 5 business days
  • Delete your account record, email, display name, licence status and AI usage counters within 30 days
  • Keep only what tax and accounting law requires us to keep about past payments, for as long as the law requires
  • Email you when it is done

Deleting your account cancels any active subscription. You can also cancel yourself in one step from your account or the renewal reminder email; no phone call or support ticket is needed.

4. What we cannot delete for you

Clips you already posted to YouTube, TikTok or anywhere else are controlled by those platforms. Delete them there. We never had a copy.

5. Questions

If anything here is unclear, email support@motionmania.app and a human will answer. See also our privacy policy.
